# Air travel and Wh limits for power banks

Watt-hours = mAh x rated voltage / 1000. A 20,000 mAh cell pack at 3.7 V is 74 Wh.

| Battery energy (Wh) | Commonly applied airline rule |
|---|---|
| up to 100 | Allowed in carry-on without airline approval |
| over 100 and up to 160 | Carry-on only with airline approval (usually max two) |
| over 160 | Not allowed on passenger aircraft |

Power banks travel in carry-on baggage only. Always confirm with your airline and see the [FAA guidance](https://www.faa.gov/hazmat/packsafe/more_info/?id=7).
